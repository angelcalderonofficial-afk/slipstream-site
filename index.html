FAQ
Is any of this real money?
No. Paper only. You start with 10 fake SOL and can reset whenever.
Do I connect a wallet?
Never. There is no connect button. Slipstream can’t touch your funds because it never asks for them.
Which terminals?
Axiom, Photon, GMGN, BullX, DexScreener and Pump.fun. The widget appears on token pages automatically.
Is it free?
Yes — all of it, copy trading included.
Where does my data live?
In your browser (chrome.storage.local). Uninstalling deletes it. Nothing syncs anywhere.
